# Code of Conduct

Nestlin welcomes respectful, good-faith participation. Contributors are expected to:

- be kind and assume good intent while discussing technical disagreements;
- focus criticism on code, evidence, and behavior rather than people;
- keep support requests reproducible, relevant, and free of private data;
- respect copyright, emulator legal boundaries, and the project's no-ROM-distribution policy;
- disclose material agent/AI assistance and remain accountable for submitted work.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate distribution of copyrighted ROMs, credential abuse, and knowingly deceptive test or compatibility claims are not acceptable.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, contact the maintainer privately through the address or reporting channel listed on the repository profile. Do not post private correspondence, credentials, or sensitive personal information in a public issue. Security vulnerabilities and credential exposure belong in [`.github/SECURITY.md`](.github/SECURITY.md).
